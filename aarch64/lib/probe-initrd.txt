r9 04-01 initrd delivery probe (D-01).
These bytes are not a filesystem image — they exist so the kernel can prove
that host-placed initrd bytes are visible via DTB /chosen
(linux,initrd-start and linux,initrd-end) on the ELF + -dtb boot path.
Delivery is qemu's generic loader plus a host-patched DTB (qemu's -initrd
never delivers for ELF kernels).  The paqfs fixture replaces this probe in
04-02.
