# Plan 01-32: Rio de-risk iteration 2 — separate the X11 login from rio startup

<objective>
Stop A proved the Mac builder and the negative control, but the rio KVM leg still fails with
`RIO-FAIL rio-not-serving`. The returned log cannot tell whether the X11 password feed failed or
the login succeeded and rio never served. This plan adds one diagnostic mode that answers exactly
that question without weakening the D-28 marker: the diagnostic bake stages a profile that creates
`/srv/r9-login-ok` and `/srv/r9-login-pid.<pid>` (kernel-wide `#s` service entries) before the rio
handoff, so the serial console proves with `ls /srv` that the login reached the profile. It also
captures the drawterm window identity and screenshots for the owner.

Outcome: either the feed is proven and the failure is localized to rio startup, or the feed is
proven broken and the next fix targets focus/typing/timing. Either way the owner gets a
decision-grade result from one more KVM run, and no later plan starts until then.
</objective>
