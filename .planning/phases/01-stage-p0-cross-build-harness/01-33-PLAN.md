# Plan 01-33: Stop-B fixes — driver registration, witness preconditions, and the q35churnshell syscall gap

<objective>
Stop B exposed four defects that prevented the KVM witness from testing anything:
the moved x86_64 driver registers `expect_before -i $spawn_id` before qemu is
spawned, so gate/gate-neg/probe/flipback abort with a Tcl error; the witness
`step_kernel` runs `--rebuild` without a prior valid build; `step_cpuauth_ref`
reads member lists from an unrealised lazy reference; and `q35churnshell` hits
`syscall 10 unimplemented`, a separate churn/boot-chain gap.

This plan fixes the driver and the two step preconditions, identifies the
syscall-10 caller with process-level evidence under TCG on the Mac, and either
fixes the churn path or records a decision-grade kernel gap. It then asks the
owner for one targeted KVM rerun of the failed steps. No marker is weakened and
no later plan starts until the rerun is clean.
</objective>
