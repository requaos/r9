# Plan 01-21: Move the aarch64 boot, smoke and tier-1 gates into r9

<objective>
This plan moves the first part of the aarch64 composed gates from Harvey's flake into r9 (D-07, HARN-08): the plain boot (`boot-r9`, with the standalone interactive driver `drive-r9.expect`), smoke and tier-1, as three scripts, three expect drivers and three flake apps. They are rewired so the arm64 image comes from r9's assembler (D-16) and fixtures are read from the read-only arm64 tree (D-19). No build or bake happens in a Harvey checkout, and lima is no longer involved. These gates run on the Mac under HVF and TCG, so this plan proves its own legs of HARN-06's aarch64 quick pass: tier-1 on HVF and TCG, then smoke on HVF.

Plan 01-30 moves the remaining two aarch64 gates (net-smoke and cpu) the same way and then runs the whole quick pass. The split keeps each plan inside the per-plan budget while every plan still moves complete gates (script, driver and app) and proves them.

Purpose: the most-used aarch64 golden checks have an r9 home and pass from it.
Output: `nix/gates/{boot-r9,smoke-r9,tier1-r9}.sh`, `gates/aarch64/{drive-r9,smoke-r9,tier1-r9}.expect`, three flake apps.
</objective>
