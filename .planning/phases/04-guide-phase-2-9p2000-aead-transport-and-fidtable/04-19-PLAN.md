## Flagged assumptions

- The torn read itself is a race between `ds`'s word-by-word clear and a relaxed eight-word read. It is not reproduced as a race on the host; the pin is on the hand-built torn image and on the rule that makes it unresolvable. Stated here so the verifier does not read the pin as a concurrency proof.
- A concurrent double insert of one `(client_id, fid_key)` from two threads at the same instant is outside WR-09's truth (a repeated insert after a fallover). Plan 04-22 puts the shard mutation and its bookkeeping under one lock; whether two racing inserts of one key into two different shards need a table-level rule is recorded in the SUMMARY for the owner, not decided here.
- Edge probe rows for WIRE-07, WIRE-09 and WIRE-10 are `unclassified` and stay unresolved; see the ledger in plan 04-18.
