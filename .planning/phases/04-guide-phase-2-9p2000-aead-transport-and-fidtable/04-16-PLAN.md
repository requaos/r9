- Commit messages carry no AI attribution line of any kind. Nothing is pushed. D-24: no owner diff stop.
- `04-WIRE-DECISIONS.md` governs P7. With P7 accepted, a sequence violation closes the connection through `fail()`; with P7 amended to drop-only, the packet is dropped, the expected counter is unchanged, the connection stays open, and the sequence tests assert that instead.
