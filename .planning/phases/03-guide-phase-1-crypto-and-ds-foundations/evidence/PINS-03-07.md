full-key verification and the over-95% fill measurement: DS-04) and
`chained-publication` (the two-phase `WRITING` publication under concurrent
claims and readers: DS-04, D-13).  Each records RED, GREEN-1, GREEN-2 and

fail there on assertions.  `empty_overflow_reads_nothing` passes against the
skeleton because `None` and `false` are the right answers for an empty
structure; it is a regression guard from here on and is not RED evidence.
