## Probe 2 — the proc map of the wedged session (identical on both kernels)

  50 exportfs | 51 rio (the session's exec rio) | 52-58 empty-args procs
  (rio's proccreate'd helpers: fsysproc, the two libdraw _ioprocs, timer)
  59 ramfs, 68/69 plumber (session collateral)
  70, 72-75 "rio"-args procs CREATED BY THE SWEEP, 76 rc (the window shell)
  71: GONE — "cat: error reading /proc/71/args: devproc: no such process"

  rio's threads (mousethread etc.) are userspace threads INSIDE pid 51
  (threadcreate adds no proc); the sweep-time proc burst (70-76) is the
  window-creation chain: winctl (threadcreate — no, see note), winshell,
  the _schedexec child, AND the NESTED RIO the window shell's rc -i
  re-execs from the profile (the ghost — plus its own helper procs), plus
  per-window ramfs/plumber collateral from the same profile run.

  Note: every login session that sourced the profile with a draw-serving
  drawterm also left a full proc group behind (ramfs + plumber x2 + rio
  x5-6 + rc) — the -G probes with the gui-x11 binary (draw served) each
  spawned a whole headless rio tree. The ps pollution this produced on
  long-lived guests is the profile defect's footprint, not a leak.
